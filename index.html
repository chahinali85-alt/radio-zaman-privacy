Radio Zaman – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Radio Zaman ("the app") is a radio app made by alcode, for iPhone and Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We, the developer, run no servers and receive no data from the app.

Information the app uses

Approximate location (optional). If you turn on the weather display and allow location access, the app reads your phone's approximate location while the app is open. It sends those coordinates to Open-Meteo over an encrypted connection to get the local weather. The location isn't stored or sent anywhere else. If you don't allow location access, everything except the weather still works.

Radio stations. To list and play stations, the app connects to the Radio Browser directory and to each radio station's stream. Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when the app connects to them.

Saved songs and YouTube. Songs you save are kept only on your phone. When you tap one to find it on YouTube, the app opens a YouTube search for the song's name in YouTube or your browser, and YouTube's privacy policy applies there.

Settings on your phone. Your favourite stations, saved songs, chosen countries, language and other settings are kept only on your phone. They're deleted when you delete the app.

Permissions

  • Location (approximate): only for the weather, as described above, and only while the app is open.
  • Running in the background: so the radio keeps playing when you leave the app or lock the phone, and in CarPlay and Android Auto.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.
